About Wallet

A simpler way to track your investments.

Wallet is designed to help you organize, monitor, and understand your investments from one simple and easy-to-use platform.

What is Wallet?

Your investments, organized in one place.

Managing multiple investments can become difficult when your money is spread across different assets and platforms.

Wallet gives you a single place to record your investments, organize them by type, and monitor their current value and performance over time.

Whether you invest in gold, funds, stocks, crypto, or other assets, Wallet helps you maintain a clear picture of your portfolio.
